Essay logic, shown with real examples

Example essay paragraphs, annotated line by line to explain why they work: the point, the evidence, the analysis and the link back to the question. Once you can see what markers reward, you can do it yourself, essay after essay.

Print did not overturn religious authority overnight; it changed who could argue about it. By the 1640s, cheap pamphlets let ordinary readers debate doctrine in their own words. This suggests authority shifted from owning the text to interpreting it, a slower and more uneven change than the "revolution" story implies, which is why the question's "to what extent" matters.
